# v1.0 cognitive-memory research acceptance

Status: **approved research direction; implementation and experimental acceptance incomplete**. Authority: [#115](https://github.com/baileyrd/Adaptive-Autonomous-Memory-Subsystem/issues/115) and the owner decision on [#105](https://github.com/baileyrd/Adaptive-Autonomous-Memory-Subsystem/issues/105#issuecomment-7122148444). This changes the order of future offline research, not historical v0.8/v0.9 acceptance results or the original MVP feature boundary.

## Research admission versus deployment

Offline retrieval/cognitive utility may be measured even when latency or RSS fails a deployment gate. The ≤5 ms added p95 and ≤2× guarded peak RSS limits remain deployment criteria on matched workloads/hardware; they must not suppress quality results. Limited execution budgets can stop an expensive experiment, but its state must be explicitly failed/resource-exhausted/not-run, never an invented quality result.

Scope isolation, temporal correctness, evidence provenance, inert retrieved instructions, and no unauthorized promotion remain mandatory. Experimental encoders/indexes are disposable and separately versioned. Canonical evidence remains immutable, beliefs explicitly revised, and model output never becomes independent evidence through repetition. Native and MCP recall retain scoped BM25 and guarded routing. Neural serving and learned-policy activation remain offline; any live activation requires a separate explicit decision and verified safety, utility and justified costs.

## Issue #115 delivery gates

1. **A — Traceability:** [31-item implementation matrix](../audit/cognitive-memory-traceability.md), code/tests/CI references, measured-versus-unknown outcomes and prioritized gaps. Only bounded behavior with direct evidence is marked verified. This PR delivers that audit and [baseline evidence](../audit/cognitive-memory-baseline.json).
2. **B — Offline retrieval research:** implement and execute the [bounded independent plan](../audit/cognitive-memory-evaluation-plan.md), retaining costly arms, failures and unexecuted work. #109 continues as the quality workstream; #107/#108 cost/placement work is complementary rather than a prerequisite. Reuse #112–#114 and preserve their review/merge state.
3. **C — Adaptive-memory task outcomes:** separately execute no-memory/raw-search/structured-memory comparisons and safety ablations. A storage restart or a retrieval label does not satisfy task continuity or learning. Missing checkpoint/sharing arms remain unimplemented rather than simulated as product features.
4. **Release evidence:** publish the four scorecards, source/tested SHA, exact commands, pinned inputs, CI/run links, negative results, uncertainty and a quality/cost Pareto disposition. #110 records the final decision. Do not close #115 or label v1.0 accepted while task-level evidence or required independent quality work is missing.

The next bounded change is #109's offline quality-runner plumbing and scorecards. This document authorizes research reporting under the already approved direction; it does not authorize product architecture expansion, missing feature implementation, model downloads in default CI, a canary, or relaxed security.
